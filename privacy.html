Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Personal Tracker is a habit and task tracker. This page explains what information it keeps and why.

What we collect

  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account's name, email address and profile picture, and a unique account ID. We use these only to sign you in and show who is signed in.
  • What you enter in the tracker: your habits, ticks, times, goals, tasks, notes, and mood, motivation, energy and focus ratings.
  • If you use it without an account: everything stays in your own browser's storage and is never sent to us.

We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and we don't ask Google for anything beyond your basic profile (name, email and picture).

How it's used

Your tracker data is used only to show it back to you on any device where you're signed in. It is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used to train anything.

Where it's stored

Accounts and tracker data are stored with Supabase, in the European Union (Frankfurt). The site itself is hosted by Vercel. Sign-in is handled by Google. Each person's data is protected so that only they can read or change it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept for as long as you use the tracker. Signing out removes the copy saved in that browser. To delete your account and all your tracker data, email YOUR-EMAIL@example.com from the address you signed in with, and it will be removed within 30 days.

You can also remove the tracker's access to your Google account at any time from your Google account settings.

Children

Personal Tracker is not intended for children under 13.
